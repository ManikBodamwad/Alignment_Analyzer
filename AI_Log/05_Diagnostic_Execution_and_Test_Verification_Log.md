## 5.2 Automated Verification Suite Mapping (All 10 Required Scenarios)

All 10 mandatory test scenarios from Section 20 of the specification are verified in [`tests/test_alignment_analyzer.py`](../tests/test_alignment_analyzer.py):

| Scenario # | Required Test Scenario | Pytest Function | Verified Behavior |
|---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | Clearly documented stakeholder policy | `test_scenario_1_and_2_documented_policy_and_new_governance_mechanism` | Extracts formal committee/policy as `FACT` with exact page & section citation. |
| **2** | Publicly announced new governance mechanism | `test_scenario_1_and_2_documented_policy_and_new_governance_mechanism` | Discovers company-specific expectations (`is_discovered_from_docs = True`) and extracts `OrganizationalUnit` records. |
| **3** | Company makes a claim without independent verification | `test_scenario_3_company_claim_without_independent_verification` | Classifies self-reported brochure statements as `COMPANY_CLAIM` and caps alignment at `Partial Alignment` (`3.0`), never `Strong Alignment` (`4.0`). |
| **4** | Two documents contain conflicting information | `test_scenario_4_conflicting_documents_detection` | Detects competing claims between company disclosures and regulatory/audit findings, flagging `is_contradictory = True`. |
| **5** | Important capability has insufficient public evidence | `test_scenario_5_insufficient_public_evidence_handling` | Classifies as `Insufficient Evidence` with `numeric_score = None` (excluded from denominator) and reduces `evidence_coverage_pct`. |
| **6** | Stakeholder has competing expectations with another stakeholder | `test_scenario_6_competing_stakeholder_expectations` | Populates `StakeholderConflict` records with Stakeholder A/B, involved units, current resolution mechanisms, weaknesses, and recommended governance. |
| **7** | New assessment period created using updated documents | `test_scenario_7_and_8_new_period_and_historical_comparison` | Creates isolated `AssessmentPeriod` without overwriting historical documents. |
| **8** | Historical assessment compared with current assessment | `test_scenario_7_and_8_new_period_and_historical_comparison` | Computes alignment delta, coverage delta, dimension shifts, new capabilities, and document date-difference advisories. |
| **9** | Document fails to process | `test_scenario_9_document_fails_to_process` | Gracefully catches corrupted PDF binary headers, sets `processing_status = "failed"`, and records `error_message`. |
| **10** | AI-generated conclusion has no supporting evidence | `test_scenario_10_validation_gate_rejects_unsupported_ai_conclusion` | Triggers `EVIDENCE VALIDATION GATE` and automatically downgrades unsupported claims to `Insufficient Evidence` (`numeric_score = None`). |
| **11** | Untrusted document prompt-injection attack | `test_prompt_injection_defense_in_uploaded_document` | Redacts adversarial instructions (`[SECURITY REDACTED...]`) and sets `prompt_injection_flag = True`. |
| **12** | Evaluator manual override & score recalculation | `test_manual_override_and_score_recalculation_with_audit_trail` | Persists `ManualOverride` and `AuditLog` with mandatory justification and recalculates overall scores. |
| **13** | 14-section report & binary exports (`PDF`, `DOCX`, `CSV`, `XLSX`) | `test_report_generation_and_binary_exports_pdf_docx_csv_xlsx` | Verifies all 14 report sections and round-trip extraction of generated `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.csv`, and `.xlsx` files. |
